# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), summarized here.

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

These standards apply in project spaces and when representing ShareKit. Report concerns privately to [hello@vladleesi.dev](mailto:hello@vladleesi.dev). Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporters' privacy, and may warn, suspend, or ban participants for violations.
